Welcome to CBAM-Myconet, On this platform you can easily submit you CBAM relevant information to your European customers.
The data is structured as follows: Goods: Here you find a list of all goods for which data is requested from you Suppliers: Here you can enter, manage and review all suppliers from which you source any CBAM-goods Installations: Here you can enter all Installations your company operates and allocate different emission values to them My Operating company: Here you can edit your company and contact informtation
Please follow these steps in order to submit your data.
  1. Please, review all goods allocated to you on the “Goods”-page
  2. Create supplier entries for all suppliers from which you have purchased CBAM-goods yourself on the “Suppliers”-page
  3. Go to the “installations”-page and enter all installations in which you manufacture CABM goods. Create all relevant emission entries for each installation.
  4. Enter you company and contact details on “My operating company”
  5. Go back to the “Goods”-list and follow this workflow:
    1. Click on the 3-dots to select an action
    2. First “reject” all items your company did not sell
    3. Next “split” all items for which you bought a partial amount from another supplier/different suppliers
      1. You can split the requested mass into as many parts as you need
      2. Please, do not split the mass by different components. The request refers to the listed product not its individual components.
      3. Splitting is only relevant, if the full sold mass stems from differently sourced batches/companies (yours can be one of these companies)
      4. You can also split the mass, if the full amount was produced in different installations, you operate
    4. Forward all amounts you bought to the corresponding supplier
    5. Assign an installation and the relevant emission values to all items produced by your company
      1. You are only responsible to enter the emission data, if you have conducted the last relevant productions step. Assembling and packaging is not considered as relevant production step. If you only assembled/paged the item, please forward the data request to the companies you purchased the individual items from. In this case a splitting by components (translated via component mass) can be correct.
  6. Submit all entries you did via xxx
